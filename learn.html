FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a decentralized digital currency that uses cryptography for security and operates on blockchain technology, independent of governments or central banks. Introduced in 2009 with Bitcoin, it marked the beginning of a peer-to-peer financial system. Since then, cryptocurrencies have transformed the modern economy by offering faster, cheaper, and more secure transactions, while also expanding financial access and driving innovation across industries.

Is cryptocurrency legal and safe?

Cryptocurrency is generally legal across much of Europe and many other regions, but its regulatory status can be unclear and is subject to change, which adds to its risk. While investing in crypto can offer high returns, it is also highly volatile and considered riskier than traditional assets like stocks or government bonds. Safety depends on how and where you invest—using secure platforms and only investing money you can afford to lose can help manage the risk.

What is blockchain?

Blockchain is the technology behind cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin. It’s a secure, transparent, and decentralised digital ledger that records transactions in a way that can’t be altered or deleted. Each transaction is grouped into a “block” and linked to the previous one, forming a tamper-proof “chain.” With its strong security and lack of central control, blockchain is revolutionising finance through crypto and paving the way for a more open and trustworthy digital future.

Can I lose money with crypto?

Yes, investing in cryptocurrencies carries risks due to their volatile nature. Prices can rise rapidly but also drop sharply within short periods. Losses can occur if investments are made without research or during market downturns. That’s why it’s important to understand the market, set investment limits, and only use money you can afford to lose. cryPIto provides educational tools and insights to help users make informed decisions.

Is cryptocurrency safe to use?

Cryptocurrency can be safe when proper precautions are taken. It uses blockchain technology, which is secure and nearly impossible to tamper with. However, users must be cautious about scams, phishing, and storing their crypto in secure wallets. Using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, and avoiding sharing private keys are essential steps to protect your assets.
